Select text on any page, click the bookmark, and the selection lands on your clipboard as Markdown.

Name

This becomes the bookmark's name. Browsers don't show icons for bookmarklets, so the emoji is how you'll spot it; shorter names won't get cut off in the bar.

Install

  1. Show your bookmarks bar (⌘⇧B on Mac, Ctrl+Shift+B on Windows).
  2. Drag this button onto the bookmarks bar:
    📋 Copy as Markdown
